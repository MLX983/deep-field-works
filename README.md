# Deep Field Works

> Exploring the transition into an AI-mediated world, one observation at a time.

Deep Field Works is a field journal, research notebook, and publishing platform for exploring how humans, technology, knowledge systems, and artificial intelligence interact.

The project serves as:

* A public notebook
* A research archive
* A prototype cognitive institution
* A development environment for ideas that may later become articles, experiments, videos, or software

The goal is not audience growth, SEO optimization, personal branding, or content marketing.

The goal is to document observations, develop ideas, preserve intellectual history, and explore emerging human-machine workflows.

## Development Philosophy

Prefer:

* Simple solutions
* Static generation
* Minimal dependencies
* Clear information architecture
* Long-term maintainability

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

The site should remain understandable and maintainable by a single individual.

---

## Publishing Philosophy

Deep Field Works is organized as a chronological archive of observations, experiments, and evolving ideas.

The archive intentionally preserves:

* Early thinking
* Incomplete models
* Dead ends
* Revisions
* Checkpoints

The evolution of thought is part of the value of the project.

---

## Long-Term Direction

Over time, Deep Field Works aims to become:

* A field journal
* A research archive
* A knowledge base
* A prototype cognitive institution

The archive is not merely documentation.

The archive itself is part of the experiment.

# Content Inbox

This folder contains pre-draft material for Deep Field Works.

Items in this folder are not published content.

They are working artifacts intended to preserve ideas before they are developed into notes, field reports, essays, experiments, concepts, or checkpoints.

## Purpose

The inbox exists to prevent useful observations, conversations, and emerging patterns from being lost before they can be evaluated.

The goal is preservation and classification, not publication.

## Content Types

Typical contents include:

* harvest candidates
* evidence packs
* article ideas
* concept candidates
* checkpoint candidates
* prototype observations
* conversation syntheses

Items may be incomplete.

That is expected.

## Lifecycle

Most material follows a path similar to:

```text
Conversation
→ Harvest Candidate
→ Evidence Pack
→ Draft
→ Review
→ Published Artifact
→ Concept / Checkpoint
```

Not every item moves through every stage.

Some candidates remain notes.

Some are merged into larger pieces.

Some are abandoned.

## Harvest Candidates

Harvest candidates are structured records of potentially valuable ideas.

They are research material, not outlines.

A harvest candidate should preserve:

* the originating question
* supporting observations
* concrete examples
* competing interpretations
* notable language
* unresolved tensions

The objective is to preserve enough context that a future human or AI agent can understand why the idea mattered.

## Evidence Packs

Evidence packs exist to preserve the reasoning path behind an idea.

Summaries often preserve conclusions while losing:

* examples
* disagreements
* analogies
* reasoning chains
* alternative interpretations

Evidence packs help retain this material for future development.

## Guidance for AI Agents

Before drafting:

1. Read the relevant source-of-truth documents.
2. Identify the candidate's artifact type.
3. Evaluate whether sufficient evidence exists.
4. Preserve uncertainty where appropriate.
5. Distinguish observation from interpretation.
6. Avoid generic AI commentary.
7. Treat harvest candidates as research material rather than publication-ready content.

If evidence is insufficient, stop and explain what is missing.

Do not invent supporting material.

## Folder Structure

Example:

```text
content-inbox/
  README.md

  harvests/
    2026-06-week-1/
      storage-is-not-memory.md

```

## Principle

The inbox is part of the cognitive infrastructure of Deep Field Works.

Its purpose is not to generate content.

Its purpose is to preserve promising ideas long enough for them to be evaluated, connected, refined, and, when justified, developed into durable public work.

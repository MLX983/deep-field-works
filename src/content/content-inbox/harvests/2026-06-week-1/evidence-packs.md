## Why It Matters

Current AI publishing systems preserve conclusions but frequently lose the reasoning, examples, and tensions that produced them.

Evidence packs may represent a missing structural layer.

## Larger Pattern It May Represent

Future human-AI publishing systems may require dedicated artifacts for preserving reasoning paths, not just outputs.

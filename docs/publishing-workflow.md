# Publishing Workflow

## Philosophy

Publish small.

The objective is continuous accumulation of observations, not production of polished masterpieces.

A short, useful observation is preferable to an unfinished perfect article.

## Draft Pipeline

Research Notes
↓
Field Note
↓
Article
↓
Checkpoint

Not every idea must pass through every stage.

Some ideas will remain notes forever.

## Before Publishing

Confirm:

* The central observation is clear.
* The article has a meaningful title.
* The article distinguishes observation from interpretation.
* The article adds something beyond summarizing news.

## After Publishing

Do not continually rewrite published work.

Minor corrections are acceptable.

The publication record should preserve the evolution of thinking.

## Content Selection

Prefer topics that are:

* Timely
* Relevant to daily life
* Evidence of broader shifts
* Useful for understanding human-machine systems

Avoid publishing content solely because it is trending.

## Long-Term Goal

Over time, the site should become:

* A field journal
* A research archive
* A knowledge base
* A prototype cognitive institution

The archive itself is part of the experiment.

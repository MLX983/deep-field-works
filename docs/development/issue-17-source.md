# Issue #17: AI consumer control surface

**URL:** https://github.com/MLX983/dfw-intake/issues/17

### Body

## The design problem

Child-level means that trust controls should be instantly understandable, not
that the user is a child. The design idea is that the user should not manage
the AI like a system administrator.

## The interaction choice

The proposed artifact is a child-level personal AI control surface called My
AI Rules. The screen answers four questions: what does the AI know, what can it
use, what can it do, and when must it ask first? The control surface
communicates what is allowed, what requires permission, and what is off-limits.

## How the control surface is grouped

### What my AI knows

- What my AI knows includes name, family, projects, schedule, likes, and writing style, with Keep, Ask first, and Forget states.

### What my AI can use

- What my AI can use includes calendar, email, notes, photos, location, and contacts, with Yes, Ask me, and No states.

### What my AI can do

- What my AI can do includes helping the user think, drafting messages, creating reminders, organizing things, suggesting plans, and talking to other apps, with Always okay, Ask first, and Never states.

### Before you act, ask me if

- Before acting, it must ask about money, family, health, work, contacting another person, and changing or deleting something.

## Design principles

- The user should be able to establish ordinary-language house rules and see that the AI is following them.

## Current state

This is a proposed screen structure. The issue does not claim that it has been
built or tested.

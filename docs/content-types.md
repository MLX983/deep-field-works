# Content Types

## Field Notes

Short observations.

Characteristics:

* Fast to publish
* Focused on a single observation
* Often triggered by current events
* May contain tentative interpretations

Typical length:

200–600 words

## Articles

More developed explorations.

Characteristics:

* Multiple observations
* Supporting examples
* Structured argument
* Open questions

Typical length:

800–2,000 words

## Checkpoints

Periodic summaries of an ongoing topic.

Purpose:

* Help readers catch up
* Consolidate previous work
* Document changes in thinking

Examples:

* AI Adoption Checkpoint
* Cognitive Institutions Checkpoint
* Agentic Systems Checkpoint

## Research Notes

Working material.

Characteristics:

* Incomplete
* Exploratory
* May contain fragments
* May later become articles

## Experiments

Descriptions of prototypes, workflows, and tests.

Characteristics:

* Explain the goal
* Explain the process
* Document results
* Document failures

## Project Journals

Progress logs.

Purpose:

* Capture development history
* Preserve decisions
* Record lessons learned

These are closer to laboratory notes than finished essays.

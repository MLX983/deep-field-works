### Body

According to a public-opinion summary, people increasingly use automated systems while remaining uncertain about their institutional legitimacy.

Working model: Adoption may demonstrate utility or dependency without demonstrating trust, accountability, or social acceptance.

Possible claim: Legitimacy could emerge through product behavior, institutional rules, public failures, and repeated ordinary use.

Open question: What did the cited survey measure, and did it distinguish adoption from trust or legitimacy?

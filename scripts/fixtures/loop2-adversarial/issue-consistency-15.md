### Body

Use proves availability, utility, and normalization.

Working model: Adoption and utility may contribute to normalization, but they do not by themselves establish that an AI-mediated process has earned rightful authority or acceptance.

Possible claim: A correct result may still arise through an illegitimate process when authority, accountability, consent, or meaningful review is absent.

Open question: What exactly requires legitimacy: a product, answer, decision, deployment, process, action, person, or institution?

### Body

Access to the model may be equal, but users differ in who can verify sources and articulate judgment. Verification becomes a form of power when one person has durable context and another has only a generic prompt window.

Review capacity can also become scarce. People differ in their ability to check outputs, maintain provenance, explain constraints, and distinguish a trustworthy source from a plausible restatement.

Source verification affects trust, but it is also an institutional capability. A useful system should help users validate claims, preserve judgment, and make review boundaries visible without treating every mention of checking as an unresolved research task.

The working tension is between broad access to model capability and unequal access to the context, review practices, and articulated judgment that make that capability useful. This is a conceptual model rather than an established empirical hierarchy.

### Body

Observation: A proposed review control should keep pending decisions visible.

Working model: A compact panel may reduce the distance between an action and its approval boundary.

Open question: What information does a reviewer need before deciding?

## The design problem

A reviewer needs to identify pending decisions without searching across several screens.

## The interaction choice

The proposed panel places a short action summary beside the available decision.

## Design principles

- Keep the decision boundary visible.
